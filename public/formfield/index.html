04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity.
Before we begin.

What do the price ranges include?+

The ranges are illustrative prices for this fictional provider. The final scope, parts, access, and finish affect a quote. Ada can explain the service options, but a site survey would be needed to confirm the work.

Is there a survey fee?+

The demo survey fee is $85 and is credited toward the work. Enquiries and a conversation with Ada are free.

When would I pay a deposit?+

The fictional booking terms require a 30% deposit after you agree the written quote and confirm a booking. This demo does not collect payments or make real bookings.

Is the workmanship covered?+

The demo terms include a 12-month workmanship warranty. Product warranties and the precise scope of coverage would be set out in a written quote.

Can Ada help with an urgent leak?+

Ada can help you describe the issue and explain the demo service. If water is actively escaping, shut off the local valve or main water supply if it is safe to do so. Keep clear of affected electrics and contact a real local professional for urgent help.
