What does the diagnostic cost?+

The diagnostic is $49, credited toward your repair. An enquiry with Kit is free. Kit can help you understand which service and price range might fit before you decide.

Are these fixed prices?+

They’re fictional demo price ranges: leak repair $110–240, faucet replacement $95–195, toilet repair $140–260, and bathroom refresh $1,950–3,450. A final scope would need to be agreed before booking.

How does booking work?+

The demo booking policy requires a 20% deposit. You can explore a plan with Kit, but this website doesn’t make real bookings or take payments.

Can I bundle a couple of jobs?+

Yes. Kit may offer a 10% bundle discount with a 24-hour expiry as part of our fictional sales policy. It’s a demo offer, with no real countdown or payment obligation. Ask Kit about the work you have in mind.

What if water is leaking right now?+

If you can do so safely, turn off the water supply and keep away from wet electrical equipment. For an urgent situation, contact an actual local repair professional. GoodHands and Kit are part of a demo and can’t dispatch anyone.
